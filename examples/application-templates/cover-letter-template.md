# Example: Cover Letter Template

A planning example of a cover letter template structure Career Hub can fill in from stored
profile data and a specific job. Placeholders in `{{double_braces}}` are filled from your
`CandidateProfile` and the target `Job` — never invented.

```
Dear {{hiring_manager_name_or_"Hiring Team"}},

I'm writing to apply for the {{job.title}} role at {{job.company}}. {{professional_summary}}

In my current/most recent role as {{most_recent_experience.title}} at
{{most_recent_experience.company}}, I {{most_recent_experience.top_achievement}}. I believe this
translates directly to what you're looking for in this role, particularly around
{{matched_requirement_1}} and {{matched_requirement_2}}.

{{closing_paragraph}}

{{candidate_name}}
{{contact_method}}
```

Every generated letter is a **draft** — see
[`docs/application-workflow.md`](../../docs/application-workflow.md) and
[`docs/profile-and-preferences.md`](../../docs/profile-and-preferences.md) ("Profile
Truthfulness Rules") — and requires your review before use.
